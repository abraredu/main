AMG001 • Group Classroom

Madrassa Group

Our structured group classroom programme organizes learners into progressive class divisions (Class 1 through Class 7+), fostering a lively and supportive Islamic learning community.

Students cover Aqeedah, Fiqh, Seerah, Akhlaq, Arabic reading and writing, and Qur'anic studies through interactive group sessions, activity-based lessons, and annual level upgrades.

Class-wise structured syllabus (Class 1 to 7+)
Interactive peer learning & group activities
Monthly parent progress report cards
Regular 90-day academic assessments
Apply for Madrassa Group
AMO001 • Individual Mentoring

Madrassa One-to-One

Our flagship individual programme pairs each student with a dedicated, qualified mentor for private live sessions tailored to their age, background, and learning pace.

Ideal for families across different global time zones, students progress confidently through the complete Madrasa curriculum with 100% personal attention.

Dedicated 1-on-1 certified Islamic mentor
Flexible timings suited to school & global time zones
Customized pace with 90-day assessment tracking
Direct parent-mentor feedback & monthly reports
Apply for Madrassa One-to-One
AT001 • Qur'an & Tajweed

Tarteel Qur'an

Focused on mastering accurate, melodious Qur'anic recitation, Tarteel Qur'an guides learners step-by-step from foundational Arabic letters and Noorani Qaida to fluent Tajweed and Hifz support.

Open to both children and adults, every session emphasizes live Makharij correction, Tajweed application, and steady memorization of essential Surahs.

Noorani Qaida to fluent Qur'an recitation
Live Makharij & Tajweed rule correction
Guided Juz' Amma & Surah memorization (Hifz)
Separate tracks for children and adult learners
Apply for Tarteel Qur'an
AS001 • Academic Tuition

School Online

Designed to help students excel in their regular school academics within a value-driven Islamic environment, School Online offers focused one-to-one tutoring across core school subjects.

Tutors provide homework assistance, concept clarity, exam preparation, and regular parent updates so students thrive both academically and spiritually.

One-to-one academic mentoring for school subjects
Homework help, concept building & exam prep
Safe, values-based online learning environment
Monthly academic progress tracking
Apply for School Online
