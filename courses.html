Individual Programme

Madrasa One-to-One

Our flagship programme pairs each student with a dedicated, qualified tutor for private, personalised sessions. The curriculum is adapted to the student's level, learning pace, and goals — ensuring maximum progress with individual attention.

This programme covers the full Madrasa syllabus including Arabic reading and writing, Islamic studies, Fiqh, and Seerah — all delivered in a structured, supportive one-to-one setting.

Personalised learning plan crafted for each student

Group Programme

Madrasa Group

Our most popular programme — small group classes of 4–8 students, fostering a sense of community while delivering structured Islamic education. Students benefit from peer learning, group activities, and a supportive classroom atmosphere.

The curriculum follows the standard Madrasa syllabus with structured assessments every term. Group classes instil discipline, social skills, and a love for learning in a community setting.

Small groups of 4–8 students for focused learning
Structured Madrasa curriculum with term assessments
Monthly progress reports shared with parents
Islamic activities, competitions and group projects
Most affordable full Madrasa programme

Tajweed Programme

Qur'an Recitation

A focused programme for students who wish to learn or improve their Qur'anic recitation with proper Tajweed (rules of recitation). From complete beginners learning the Qa'idah (alphabet) to advanced students completing the full Qur'an — everyone is welcome.

Our certified Tajweed teachers bring warmth and expertise to every session, correcting pronunciation with patience and encouraging students to develop a lifelong love for the Qur'an.

Suitable for all ages — from 4 years to adults
From Qa'idah beginners to advanced Hifz preparation
Certified Tajweed teachers
Individual and small group sessions available
Most accessible entry-level Islamic programme
