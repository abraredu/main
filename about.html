Our Story & Vision

About ABRAR Online Islamic School

A community-driven institution dedicated to authentic Islamic education, built on trust, tradition, and a commitment to excellence.

Our Purpose

Inspiring Lifelong Learning Through Faith & Knowledge

At Abrar, we believe that Islamic education is not merely about completing lessons—it's about nurturing faith, building character, and developing a lifelong connection with the Qur'an and Islamic values.

Our Mission

To make authentic Islamic education accessible to learners of all ages through live online learning, structured programmes, and dedicated mentoring, enabling every student to grow in faith, knowledge, and character.

Our Vision

To become a trusted global online Islamic learning community that inspires individuals and families to live by the teachings of Islam through quality education, sincere mentoring, and a love for the Qur'an.

Our Learning Philosophy

We believe every learner is unique. That's why we focus on personal mentoring, consistent encouragement, structured learning, and continuous progress tracking. Our goal is not only to teach Islamic knowledge, but to nurture confident Muslims who practise their faith with understanding, sincerity, and good character.

Our Core Values

What We Stand For

These values guide every decision we make, every class we teach, and every interaction we have with our students and families.

Integrity

We operate with complete honesty and transparency — in our teaching, our fees, and our communication with families.

Excellence

We hold ourselves to the highest standards in Islamic scholarship and teaching, continuously improving our methods.

Compassion

Every student is treated with care, patience and respect — we celebrate effort and progress, not just results.

Community

We exist to serve our community — bringing families together and strengthening the bonds of our Ummah.

Our Journey

How We Grew

From a small study circle to a structured institution — our journey has been one of faith, dedication and community support.

2019

Founded

ABRAR Madrasa was established with just 15 students, 2 tutors and a simple vision — accessible Islamic education for all.

2020

First Graduates

Our first cohort of students completed the foundational Madrasa curriculum, a proud milestone for the institution and their families.

2022

Digital Transformation

We launched the ABRAR Management System — a digital platform for student progress tracking, fee management, and parent communication.

2023

Qur'an Programme

Launched the dedicated Qur'an Recitation programme with Tajweed focus, welcoming students of all ages from complete beginners.

2025

Growing Strong

Today we serve 150+ students with 12+ qualified tutors, running morning and evening sessions across three distinct programmes.
