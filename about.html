🕌 Our Story

About ABRAR Madrasa

A community-driven institution dedicated to authentic Islamic education, built on trust, tradition and a commitment to excellence.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To provide every Muslim child and adult in our community with access to authentic, high-quality Islamic education — delivered through qualified tutors, structured programmes, and a nurturing learning environment.

🌟 Our Vision

To become the leading centre for Islamic education in the region — where students graduate with strong faith, deep knowledge, and the character to contribute positively to their communities and the Ummah.

📿 Our Approach

We blend traditional Islamic scholarship with modern pedagogy — ensuring lessons are engaging, structured, and effective. Progress is tracked digitally, and parents receive monthly reports to stay connected with their child's journey.

Our Journey

How We Grew

From a small study circle to a structured institution — our journey has been one of faith, dedication and community support.

2019

Founded

ABRAR Madrasa was established with just 15 students, 2 tutors and a simple vision — accessible Islamic education for all.

2020

First Graduates

Our first cohort of students completed the foundational Madrasa curriculum, a proud milestone for the institution and their families.

2022

Digital Transformation

We launched the ABRAR Management System — a digital platform for student progress tracking, fee management, and parent communication.

2023

Qur'an Programme

Launched the dedicated Qur'an Recitation programme with Tajweed focus, welcoming students of all ages from complete beginners.

2025

Growing Strong

Today we serve 150+ students with 12+ qualified tutors, running morning and evening sessions across three distinct programmes.
